Prove you own a {{ panel.asset }} address

This proves that you hold the key to a {{ panel.asset }} address, by having you sign a one-time message with your own wallet. It never asks for a private key, a seed phrase or a wallet passphrase, and it never will. Everything secret stays on your machine: you sign there, and only the signature travels.

What it does not do: proving an address here does not change your payout address, your price, your amount, or anything else about swap {{ panel.swap_id }}. It is a record that you control the address, nothing more.

  2. Sign it in your own wallet, on your own machine. In the Gridcoin client's console, replacing the placeholder with the address you want to prove: {{ panel.sign_command }} In the graphical client it is File → Sign message: put your address in the first box, the challenge in the second, and press Sign. Nothing from this step leaves your machine except the signature it prints. We never see your key, and we do not want to.
  3. Paste the address and the signature back here. The signature is a long base64 string, usually ending in =. If what you are about to paste looks like a key — 51 or 52 characters, or 64 hex digits, or a row of words — stop. That is the secret, not the signature. This page will refuse it, but the safer move is not to paste it anywhere.
