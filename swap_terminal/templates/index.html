Swap one chain for another

Send the coin you have to an address derived for your swap alone. When it has enough confirmations, the coin you want goes to your address. One quote, one deposit, one payout.

What can be swapped right now

Read from Config.ALLOWED_PAIRS and from the chain adapters this server process actually built, when this page was built. A pair needs both: ALLOWED_PAIRS is what this terminal is willing to swap, and an adapter is what it can reach. A pair marked DISABLED is allowed but unreachable — the reason beside it says what to set. The form below offers only the enabled ones.

2 Create the swap

Creating a swap fixes where your payout goes. The address is checked with the destination chain's own daemon first, and it cannot be changed afterward — a payout is final the moment it is broadcast.
