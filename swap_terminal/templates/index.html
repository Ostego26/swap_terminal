2 Create the swap

Creating a swap fixes where your payout goes. The address is checked with the destination chain's own daemon first, and it cannot be changed afterward — a payout is final the moment it is broadcast.

(no swap yet) A quote has to exist first, so the button above stays disabled until one does. Creating the swap derives a deposit address and takes you to a page that tracks it.

Already have a swap?

Every swap has its own page at /swap/<id>. It is a plain link — bookmark it, close the tab, come back hours later. Amounts outside the {{ (tolerance_pct * 100) | round(2) }}% tolerance band are held for a human rather than paid out.
