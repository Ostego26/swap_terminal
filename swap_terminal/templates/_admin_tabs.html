Before this, /admin/controls was reachable only through a sentence in the middle of /admin's prose, and /admin was reachable from it only through a breadcrumb -- two pages that are one surface, joined by text. TABS AND NOT A MERGE, and the distinction is a safety property rather than a layout preference. /admin opens "Operator surface - read-only" and means it: its own header records that there is NO form on that page which posts anywhere. The controls page exists to arm a payout worker that broadcasts. Folding the second into the first would put a start button on the page whose stated guarantee is that nothing on it changes anything -- and that guarantee is load-bearing, because it is what makes the dashboard safe to leave open. The operator asked for "tabs or hyperlinks", so the ask is satisfied without spending it. WHY THE CONTROLS PAGE STAYS A SEPARATE DOCUMENT AT ALL, from its own header: "the moment you need a stop button is the moment something on this host is already misbehaving and the big page may be the thing that is slow." A tab is a link; the direct URL still works and is still bookmarkable. THE CANISTER CONSOLE IS NAMED AND NOT LINKED, which is the honest option. It is served by the operator_admin canister at http://.localhost:4943/, and this process cannot know that id: every fresh replica issues different ones, the id lives in the replica's own state, and nothing mounts it into this container. `swap_stack.py status` asks the replica and prints the URL. A tab pointing at a guessed id would link to another deployment (rule 17). THE THIRD TAB, 2026-10-10. Operator: "this should be a tab under the admin and 6 sub tabs for each chain." Chain wallets is a tab HERE and its six chains are sub-tabs INSIDE that page, which is the shape they asked for and also the only shape that works: six chains in this strip would put two surfaces and six chain panels on one row, and a strip eight items long stops being a way to find a surface. IT GOES BESIDE System state AND NOT INSIDE IT, for a different reason than the controls page's and it lands in the same place. The chain panel is as read-only as /admin is, so there is no guarantee to spend here; what it has is a SOCKET. /admin renders from the database and contacts nothing, deliberately, and folding in a page that can spend twenty seconds waiting on a daemon would make the dashboard an operator leaves open as slow as the slowest chain. Separate documents, so the fast page stays fast. AND ITS TAB POINTS AT THE BARE PATH, not at a chain. Which chain comes first is services/chain_panel.panel_assets()' answer and it depends on Config.RPC, which a macro cannot read -- so /admin/wallets means "the first panel" and named_or_first() is where that is decided.
